About Me

Hi! I am a second-year Engineering Physics student at the University of British Columbia, and a member of the Battery-Management System (BMS) team for UBC Formula Electric.

Through my work so far on the BMS and personal robotics projects, I have built experience with embedded software principles (RTOS, state machines), communication protocols (I2C, CAN) and control loops.

In my first year of school I was on the Formula Electric Aerodynamics team, where I learned mechanical design practices in CAD and took on an aerodynamic simulation side project using Python.

I'm at my best in team environments, and excel at systematic problem solving and clear communication.

Outside of school and projects, I play too many sports (skiing, hockey, volleyball, mountain + road biking, golf). I also love listening to music and playing instruments, learning new languages, and being outdoors.
